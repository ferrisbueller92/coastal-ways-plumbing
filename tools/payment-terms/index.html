How payment works on jobs of $5,000 and over

On larger jobs the price is paid across the work rather than all at once. Nothing here changes the price you were quoted — it sets out when each part of it falls due, and what has to be finished before it does.

The payments three, in this order
1

Deposit on accepting the quote

10% of the quoted price, payable when you accept the quote and before work starts. It holds your place in the schedule and covers ordering your materials.

2

Progress invoices as the work is done

Where a job runs across more than one stage, each stage is invoiced as it is finished — for the work completed to that point, with photographs attached. The next stage begins once that invoice is paid. A job done in a single visit has none of these.

3

Final invoice on completion

The balance of the quoted price, invoiced once the work is complete and tested. Together the payments come to the quoted price and no more, unless a variation was agreed in writing.

10%

Why the deposit is small. In New South Wales a deposit on residential building work cannot exceed 10% of the contract price, however large the job. The payments in between are not capped — so the money follows the work rather than arriving up front. Every payment after the deposit must be for work genuinely carried out, not for a date on a calendar.

The terms the same on every job

When each invoice is due

Within 7 days of the invoice date.

How to pay

Bank transfer. Account details are on every invoice.

No interest, no fees

There is no interest and no fee for paying this way, and nothing built into the price for it. Paying across stages costs exactly the same as paying in one amount.

Changes to the work

Anything outside the quoted scope is agreed in writing before it is carried out, with its effect on the price set out at the same time. No surprises on the final invoice.

What this page is. It sets out how and when the quoted price is paid, and forms part of the written contract for your work — it is not the contract on its own. In NSW, residential building work over $5,000 including GST requires a written contract; these terms cover work up to $20,000, and jobs above that value are quoted with their own payment schedule. Nothing here limits the consumer guarantees under the Australian Consumer Law or the statutory warranties under the Home Building Act 1989 (NSW), which cannot be excluded.
